The case

Roohi is twenty six. Her first posting is a town in Rajasthan where the temple is larger than the police station.

A letter arrives with no name on it. It accuses the man the whole town calls Baba of what he has been doing to girls at the ashram for forty years.

Eleven people tell her it is true.

Not one of them will write it down.
